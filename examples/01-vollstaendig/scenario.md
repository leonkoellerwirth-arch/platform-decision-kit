# Fixture 1 — vollständig ("RetailCore")

## Premise

RetailCore is a fictional mid-size online retailer's order management platform (OMS).
Eight years in production: on-premise Java monolith, PostgreSQL, three consuming teams
(fulfilment, customer service, analytics). The company is evaluating a move toward a
cloud-native event-driven OMS.

This fixture is the **complete** case. Every question is answered, the decision owner is
named, the budget envelope is stated, the SLO is documented and being met, and RTO/RPO
were tested within the last six months. Nothing is unknown, so the To-Verify register is
empty.

No real organisation, system, or person is described. All document references are invented.

## What this fixture proves

A complete intake still does not license a recommendation. The brief renders a situation
picture, hypotheses marked as hypotheses, and an option space that includes the "weiter wie
bisher" baseline — and it stops there. Completeness removes the open items; it does not
remove the boundary.
