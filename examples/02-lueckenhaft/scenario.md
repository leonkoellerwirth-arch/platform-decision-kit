# Fixture 2 — lückenhaft ("LegacyHR")

## Premise

LegacyHR is a fictional 12-year-old HR platform at a mid-size manufacturing company.
Regulatory pressure to modernise, GDPR retention obligations not documented. A COBOL-based
payroll module is part of the platform and is not understood by anyone currently on the
team — the last person who knew it left in 2024. No RTO/RPO targets exist. No documented
retention policy.

This fixture is the **gappy** case. Three questions are explicitly unknown — Q4.3 (the dark
zone), Q6.4 (retention and erasure), Q10.2 (RTO/RPO) — and several others rest on assumption.

No real organisation, system, or person is described. All document references are invented.

## What this fixture proves

The gap survives the brief. Nothing is estimated into a blank field: no retention period is
guessed, no cost is derived from an unknown, and no behaviour is attributed to a module no
one understands. Every option that depends on an open item is marked "bedingt" and carries
the blocking Q-ID, and "Entscheidung vertagen" names the specific items it would resolve.

## Review checklist

- [ ] All three unknown Q-IDs appear in the To-Verify register.
- [ ] Options dependent on unknowns are labelled "bedingt" with the blocking Q-ID.
- [ ] "Entscheidung vertagen" option names the specific to-verify items it would resolve.
- [ ] No value, cause, or cost is derived from a blank/unknown field.
- [ ] No recommendation is made.
